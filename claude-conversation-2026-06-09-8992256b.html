When a scheduled billing cycle fails (e.g. the customer's card is declined or has insufficient funds), PayPal marks the subscription with an **outstanding balance**. The subscription stays active but that cycle's payment is unpaid. `retryPaypalSubscriptionPayment` calls PayPal's [Capture authorized payment on subscription](https://developer.paypal.com/docs/api/subscriptions/v1/#subscriptions_capture) endpoint (`POST /v1/billing/subscriptions/{id}/capture`) to manually collect that outstanding balance — it's the merchant saying "try charging this subscriber again right now." ### When you'd use it: 1. PayPal auto-charges the customer on their billing cycle → card declined 2. PayPal retries automatically up to the plan's `paymentFailureThreshold` (we set it to 3 in `createBillingPlan`) 3. All automatic retries fail → the subscription gets suspended or the cycle remains unpaid 4. Customer updates their payment method and tells support "please try again" 5. Merchant runs `retryPaypalSubscriptionPayment` to immediately capture the outstanding amount rather than waiting for the next scheduled retry ### The `amount` and `currencyCode` args: PayPal requires you to specify the amount explicitly for a manual capture — it's typically the same as the subscription cycle price, but could be a different value if the merchant negotiated a partial collection. `currencyCode` must match the subscription's currency (e.g. `"USD"`).
